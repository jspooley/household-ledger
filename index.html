Household Ledger

A private household budgeting app

Household Ledger helps one household download checking and credit-card activity, classify bills versus discretionary spending, and see remaining cash for the month.

Who this is for

This is a personal application used only by the household that operates it. It is not a public product, marketplace, or service offered to other customers.

Reason for data access

We access account balances and transactions so we can classify recurring bills versus discretionary spending and project remaining cash. Data stays on this household's computer and is not sold.

What data we access

Through Plaid, Household Ledger may receive:

  • Account names, types, masks, and current balances
  • Transaction dates, descriptions, amounts, and pending status
  • Institution names needed to keep checking and cards identified

We do not request the ability to move money or to initiate payments.

How the data is used

Downloaded activity is used only to:

  • Show current checking and card balances
  • Identify repeating deposits and bills
  • Track discretionary spending against a household budget
  • Estimate remaining cash for the current month

Where data is stored

Household Ledger runs on the owner's computer. Account connections and transactions are stored locally on that machine. They are not uploaded to a Household Ledger website or shared with advertisers.

Who data is shared with

Bank-login and account-aggregation are handled by Plaid and the financial institutions you choose to connect, such as a credit union or credit-card issuer. Household Ledger does not sell personal or financial data.

Retention and control

Data remains until the household deletes it in the app or disconnects the bank in Plaid Portal. Disconnecting a bank stops future downloads from that institution.

Children

Household Ledger is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended to collect their data.
